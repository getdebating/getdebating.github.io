Why debating matters

Debating is practice for real life. Students learn to back up what they think, to listen to the other side, and to change someone's mind without raising their voice. That confidence carries into the classroom, into interviews and well beyond school.

  • Think it through.
  • Say it clearly.
  • Hear the other side.

How it works

Three steps to a debating class

  1. Book a consultation

    Tell us about your school, your students and what you want them to get from debating.

  2. We recommend a programme

    We suggest the programme that suits your year group, class size and experience. Not sure what you need? We will advise.

  3. Our coaches come to you

    Experienced university debaters teach the classes in your school.
