This is a placeholder post. It is here to show how a post looks and how to write one. Nothing in it is real news. ## Writing a post Type or paste plain text. Leave a blank line between paragraphs and the site does the rest. A line that starts with two hash signs becomes a subheading, like the one above. ## Lists, bold and links Start a line with a dash to make a bullet point: - Keep paragraphs short. - One idea per paragraph. - Finish with something the reader can do next. Put two stars either side of a word to make it **bold**. To add a link, put the words in square brackets and the address in round brackets straight after, like this: [see our programmes](https://example.com). ## Before you publish Change the title, date and author at the top of the file. Then add the post to the list in blog/index.html so readers can find it.
